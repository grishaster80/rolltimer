Privacy policy

Roll Timer — BJJ & Rounds · effective 2026-10-31

Roll Timer does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your timers (names, round and rest lengths, sound choices), your app settings and the state of a running session are saved only on your device, so the timer can survive the app being closed or the phone restarting. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no internet features and makes no network requests of its own.

If you have device backups switched on (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your operating system may include these files in your own backup. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies; we never see them.

Watch apps

If you use Roll Timer on Apple Watch or Wear OS, your phone sends your timer list and the state of the running session to your own watch, and the watch sends back what you tap there (start, pause, skip, end). This goes only between your phone and your watch, through the system's watch connection (Apple WatchConnectivity on iPhone and Apple Watch, Google Play services on Android and Wear OS). Nothing reaches us. You can switch it off in the app's settings (Watch connection).

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show the running timer on the lock screen (Live Activity on iPhone, an ongoing notification on Android) and, on iPhone, a backup banner for a cue the app could not play. Optional: the timer works without it.
  • Android: foreground service, wake lock, vibration — to keep the timer accurate with the screen off and to vibrate on round changes if you enable haptics.
  • Background audio (iPhone) — to play the round, rest and countdown sounds while the phone is locked during a workout you started.
  • Android: exact alarms — only to start a workout you start from your watch while the phone app is in the background.
  • Wear OS: notifications, vibration — to keep the phone's timer on your watch face and to tap your wrist when a round or rest starts.

None of these give the app access to your contacts, location, photos, health data or any other personal information.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries. The Android and Wear OS apps use Google Play services only to connect your phone and your watch. The App Store and Google Play may collect data about downloads under their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
